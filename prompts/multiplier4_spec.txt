DESIGN: 4-BIT UNSIGNED MULTIPLIER

INPUTS:
A: 4-bit unsigned input.
B: 4-bit unsigned input.

OUTPUT:
Product: 8-bit unsigned multiplication result.

FUNCTION:
Product = A * B.

REQUIREMENTS:
1. Product must equal A multiplied by B.
2. The output must support the full 8-bit result.
3. Minimum result: 0.
4. Maximum result: 225 (15 * 15).

DESIGN TYPE:
Combinational logic.
No clock.
No reset.
