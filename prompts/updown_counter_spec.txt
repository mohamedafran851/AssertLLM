DESIGN: 4-bit synchronous up/down counter

CLOCK:
- Signal name: clk
- Positive edge triggered

RESET:
- Signal name: reset
- Active-high synchronous reset
- When reset is high, count must become zero.

COUNTER BEHAVIOUR:
- Counter is 4 bits wide.
- When reset is low and direction is 1,
  count increments by one on every rising clock edge.
- When reset is low and direction is 0,
  count decrements by one on every rising clock edge.
- The counter wraps around naturally:
  15 + 1 = 0
  0 - 1 = 15

EXPECTED VERIFICATION:
- Check synchronous reset behaviour.
- Check increment behaviour when direction is 1.
- Check decrement behaviour when direction is 0.
- Use 4-bit arithmetic for wraparound.
