DESIGN: 4-BIT COMBINATIONAL ALU

INPUTS:
A: 4-bit unsigned input.
B: 4-bit unsigned input.
ALU_Sel: 3-bit operation selector.

OUTPUTS:
ALU_Out: 4-bit result.
CarryOut: 1-bit carry output.

OPERATIONS:

1. ADD:
ALU_Sel = 000
ALU_Out and CarryOut together represent A + B.
The result is 5 bits.

2. SUB:
ALU_Sel = 001
ALU_Out and CarryOut together represent
the 5-bit unsigned result of A - B.

3. AND:
ALU_Sel = 010
ALU_Out = A & B.
CarryOut = 0.

4. OR:
ALU_Sel = 011
ALU_Out = A | B.
CarryOut = 0.

5. XOR:
ALU_Sel = 100
ALU_Out = A ^ B.
CarryOut = 0.

6. NOT:
ALU_Sel = 101
ALU_Out = ~A.
CarryOut = 0.

7. INVALID OPERATIONS:
ALU_Sel = 110 or 111
ALU_Out = 0000.
CarryOut = 0.

DESIGN TYPE:
Combinational logic.
No reset.
No internal clock.
