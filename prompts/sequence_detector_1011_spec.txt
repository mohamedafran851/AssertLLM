Design a 5-state Moore finite state machine sequence detector.

The detector must recognize the serial bit pattern 1011.
Inputs:
- clk: clock
- reset: synchronous active-high reset
- din: serial input bit

Output:
- detected: becomes 1 when the sequence 1011 is detected.

Requirements:
1. Detect the sequence 1011.
2. Support overlapping sequence detection.
3. Keep detected high for the detection state.
4. Reset must return the FSM to its initial state and clear detected.
5. Use the five states S0, S1, S2, S3, and S4.
